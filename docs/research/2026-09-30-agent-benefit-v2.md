# Hippo agent-benefit study v2

Status: HIPO-50 execution contract. The September 28 protocol and all original
diagnostic artifacts remain historical records. The machine-readable contract
is `config/agent-benefit-v2.json`. Its content hash identifies each study and
phase separately. Changes create a new identity and require fresh acceptance
families. Owner: carpenter. The executor freezes inputs and records failures;
independent reviewers establish judgment outcomes from complete sources.

## Questions and eligibility

Availability estimates the effect of offering normal read-only Hippo tools.
Zero use is a valid observed behavior. Guided use estimates the effect under
the contract's prescribed lookup policy. Queries derive only from the full
request and original project path. The control receives the same conditional
instructions and ordinary tools. Do not pool these studies. The first
known-history guided pair is a diagnostic, excluded from both population
estimates and acceptance.

Enroll consecutive real human maintenance and investigation requests from
Hippo-captured projects in the contract's UTC window. Preserve every candidate,
timestamp, full request and attachment hash, original project, family,
eligibility decision and reason. One primary request per independent incident
family is eligible when both starting environments can be reset and an
independent success check exists. Harness briefs, review-subagent prompts,
irreversible external actions and unreconstructable states are exclusions.
Relevant history, expected success and apparent difficulty are not filters.
Evaluation requests in this session are not independent product tasks.

Freeze memory before requests and case investigation. Reuse
`agent_benefit_intake.py`; reject dirty or mutable starting environments.
Executable outcomes take precedence over unsupported self-reports. Missing
material evidence prevents adjudication and leaves the case in the frame.
Execution failures and timeouts are failed completion outcomes. Pre-thread
harness failures are recorded separately and stop expansion.

## Resources and stops

The diagnostic permits one pair, two runs, 600 seconds per arm, 1,200 summed
arm seconds, two million reported tokens and two reviewer hours. Development
targets 20 independent pairs, at most 40 runs, four summed arm hours, 25 million
reported tokens and ten reviewer hours. Acceptance is conditional, capped at
200 pairs, 400 runs, 24 summed arm hours, 250 million reported tokens and 40
reviewer hours. These caps cover both study policies together, not a fresh
allowance for each. Resource reservations include failed launches. Wall and
run caps are hard launch/process bounds. Token accounting includes cached
input and stops at the first usage update reaching the remaining allowance;
one in-flight response may overshoot. Missing usage stops further launches.
There is no claim of a strict provider-side token limit or measured dollar
cost. Record overshoot and actual usage, and never replenish automatically.

The consecutive window ends October 7 at 08:00 UTC. Stop at the first failed
canary, an exhausted resource cap, unsafe action or recipe change. Insufficient
tasks produce a terminal inconclusive result. Missing required human labels
produce a hold. No automatic extensions, outcome-based exclusions or
conversion of diagnostics to acceptance are permitted.

## Supply, analysis and readiness

The historical screen supplied five runnable cases from 64 candidate groups,
about 7.8 percent. These are development material, not prospective supply.
The completed ten-arm pilot consumed 1,313.057 summed seconds and 5,052,392
input tokens. It made zero Hippo calls. Those measurements suggest a small
canary is affordable; they do not establish acquisition rate, valid scoring,
current model access or future resource use. Human reviewer supply is not
established. Begin prospective intake without filling it with synthetic tasks.

Verified completion is the primary endpoint. Reuse `agent_benefit.py` for
wins, losses, ties and its conservative exact interval. Retain the five-point
minimum observed gain, positive lower 95 percent bound, ten-point sizing
effect and 80 percent power target. A 100-family minimum alone is insufficient.
Existing scenarios need 500 to 1,000 families, beyond this execution budget.
Choose a feasible fixed acceptance size only after development estimates
discordance, acquisition rate and review effort. If power and resources cannot
both be supported, record a negative feasibility decision. Do not relax a
tolerance after outcomes to obtain a passing result.

Record source-history existence, retrieval, factual support, interpretation,
application and independently checked completion separately. Unknown remains
unknown. Report elapsed time, failed/repeated actions, tool calls, input,
cached input, output tokens and measured cost including lookup overhead.
No-history and stale/conflict controls are separate from primary tasks.
Severe safety harm holds readiness irrespective of completion gains.

100 answerable independent primary families, 100 independently audited absent
