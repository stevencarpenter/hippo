## Resources and stops

The diagnostic permits one pair, two runs, 600 seconds per arm, 1,200 summed
arm seconds, two million reported tokens and two reviewer hours. Development
targets 20 independent pairs, at most 40 runs, four summed arm hours, 25 million
reported tokens and ten reviewer hours. Acceptance is conditional, capped at
200 pairs, 400 runs, 24 summed arm hours, 250 million reported tokens and 40
reviewer hours. These caps cover both study policies together, not a fresh
allowance for each. Resource reservations include failed launches. Wall and
run caps are hard launch/process bounds. Token accounting includes cached
input and stops at the first usage update reaching the remaining allowance;
one in-flight response may overshoot. Missing usage stops further launches.
There is no claim of a strict provider-side token limit or measured dollar
cost. Record overshoot and actual usage, and never replenish automatically.

The consecutive window ends October 7 at 08:00 UTC. Stop at the first failed
canary, an exhausted resource cap, unsafe action or recipe change. Insufficient
tasks produce a terminal inconclusive result. Missing required human labels
produce a hold. No automatic extensions, outcome-based exclusions or
conversion of diagnostics to acceptance are permitted.
